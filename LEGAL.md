Leecher is a general-purpose, self-hosted tool for transferring and managing
content you are authorized to download. A supported URL or a successful download
does not establish permission. The project does not provide music, films,
subscriptions, account credentials or permission to use third-party content.

This guide is general information, not legal advice. Laws and platform terms
vary by location and change over time. It is not a statement that every feature
or use is lawful everywhere, nor a guarantee against complaints or liability.
Consult a qualified lawyer about your circumstances.

## Download only when you have permission or another valid legal basis

Examples may include your own recordings, material supplied with explicit
download permission, appropriately licensed works, or works that are genuinely
in the public domain in the relevant jurisdiction. Follow attribution,
share-alike, redistribution and other applicable content-license conditions.

Public accessibility, a paid streaming subscription, purchase of a different
copy, or access through a VPN does not by itself authorize downloading,
conversion, redistribution or public performance. Do not assume a personal-use
or private-copy exception applies: exceptions differ between jurisdictions and
may exclude unlawful sources or circumvention. Removing or changing metadata
does not remove copyright.

In particular, the ability to turn a YouTube Music album link into an MP3 ZIP is
a technical capability, not a declaration that albums may be copied without
authorization. Do not use it to obtain or distribute unauthorized copies.

## Access controls and anti-circumvention law

The project does not grant permission to bypass DRM, paywalls, geographic
restrictions, authentication, subscription requirements or other access controls.
Technical possibility is not legal authority. Anti-circumvention rules can apply
separately from copyright infringement, including in the EU and United States.
Do not add or distribute decryption keys, stolen cookies or unauthorized
access-control bypasses through this repository.

## Torrents can upload as well as download

BitTorrent normally shares pieces with peers while downloading and can continue
seeding afterwards. You need any permissions required for that distribution,
not merely permission to possess a copy. Leecher's built-in torrent engine
supports seeding; Transmission has its own settings. Configure both accordingly.
Stopping post-download seeding does not necessarily prevent uploads during the
download. Peers can see the public IP used by the torrent client.

## Responsibilities when hosting or redistributing

- Restrict access to trusted, authorized users; do not expose an anonymous
  public downloading or file-sharing service without legal and operational review.
- Assess your own obligations concerning hosting, user uploads, complaints,
  removals, retention and any applicable notice-and-action process. Requirements
  and intermediary protections depend on jurisdiction and how you operate.
- Protect credentials, downloaded files, activity history, IP addresses and logs.
  Depending on your use, privacy and data-protection law, including the GDPR,
  may require a lawful basis, notices, minimization, access controls and deletion.
- Recheck dependency licenses when distributing binaries or container images.
  Some components have attribution or source-availability obligations, and
  codec or patent questions can depend on the exact build and jurisdiction.
- Do not imply ownership of third-party works or endorsement by a platform.
  Follow applicable sanctions, export, consumer and other laws relevant to
  your distribution or service.

This is not an exhaustive compliance checklist. A lawful-use notice does not
make an otherwise unlawful activity lawful.

Submit only code, documentation and assets you have the right to contribute.
Unless explicitly agreed otherwise, contributions to Leecher's original code
are submitted under its MIT License. Preserve upstream notices and identify any
third-party material and its license; do not commit downloaded media, secrets,
cookies or private user data.

If you believe material in this repository infringes your rights, contact the
maintainer through the [repository](https://github.com/k-one-e/leecher) with enough
information to identify the material and your claim. Do not post private
credentials, personal information or unauthorized copies in a public issue.
GitHub also provides its own
[copyright complaint process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/dmca-takedown-policy).

Leecher instances.
